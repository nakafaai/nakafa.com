UTBK-SNBT sections follow their official subtest names. Section rows use icons
keyed by the new section keys, and try-out subjects and subtests own their icons
beside the try-out catalog instead of in the curriculum material map. Retired
section URLs redirect permanently to their successors once the signed catalog
serves them, while a URL that carries an attempt stays on the route its
attempt was frozen to. The dictionaries lose the stale `Exercises` namespace,
which kept a second, outdated copy of the section names; the try-out runtime
labels it still served now live in `Tryouts`.
