Redraw all 65 profile animals so each reads at once, even as small as a line
of text. Every animal now fills its circle with one larger head, has plain dots
for eyes with no shine and no cheeks, as Nina has, and wears one clear body
colour over one second colour for the parts that name it, such as a muzzle, a
mane, or a shell. Animals that did not read as themselves take a new
silhouette: the crocodile and the clownfish turn three quarters on, the komodo
gets its long head and forked tongue, the rhino its horn on the nose, and the
elephant a trunk that stands out from its face. Names, palettes, grounds,
poses and eyes keep their meaning, so every stored mascot code draws the same
animal in its new drawing.
