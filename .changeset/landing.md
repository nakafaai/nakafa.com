Let the island's weather land. Rain and snow fall along the wind and stop on
the first roof, crown or water over the ground, so the ground under a roof
stays dry; raindrops splash on roofs and ground and ring on water. The ground
darkens as it soaks and dries again in the sun, and snow settles in drifts on
everything that faces the sky, then melts once it stops. A storm's lightning
flashes over the island, and its thunder follows as late as the strike was far.
