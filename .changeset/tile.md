Draw the tile of a lesson, a material, and a try-out as one calm blend with one
white icon. The seed of a tile picks one hue out of 360, a second tone 34
degrees beside it, and one of eight directions, at one lightness and one
chroma, so every tile belongs to one family and the icon keeps a contrast of
3:1 on all of them. The same seed always gives the same tile. The home page
rows, the catalog cards, and the try-out lists use it, and the old gradient
block with its six colour schemes, three kinds of gradient, and three
intensities is gone.
