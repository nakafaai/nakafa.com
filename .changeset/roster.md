Let a school's Admin set up its periods, class groups, and people. An Admin creates a period on the school's own clock, opens it, and archives it when it ends, and only one period of a school is active at a time. An Admin forms cohorts in a period, places each person in one of them per period, and moves them between cohorts of that period. An Admin creates, renames, and removes people, and invites them to an address on one of the school's allowed email domains. An invite waits for 14 days, and an Admin may send the same invite again at most three times an hour. Removing a person ends their roles and placements and revokes their invite. Deleting an account releases its people, so they can be invited again.
