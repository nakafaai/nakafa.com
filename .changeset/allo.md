Draw every page, document and social image in Allo, rounded, Nakafa's new text
face. It reads warm without turning childish, keeps the old face's vertical
metrics so nothing moves, and its Latin file is a third lighter (32.6 KB). Text
draws at 400, 500 and 600, and the fallback faces match Allo's width, so the
swap moves no line.
