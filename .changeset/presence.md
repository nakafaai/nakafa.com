Teachers in one exam's editor see each other in its header, the way Canva and Google Docs show co-editors: an avatar for each teacher, with a tooltip that names them, and a circle that counts the rest past four. The official `@convex-dev/presence` component keeps the room. Only a member who may edit the exam joins it or reads it, an editor beats every 30 seconds while its tab shows, and a tab left in the background leaves the room, so it costs nothing.
